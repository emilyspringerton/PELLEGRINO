Why it works

Decades of defensive engineering—sandboxing, constant context switches, and heavy tracking wrappers—created layers of abstraction. Layers have a cost. PELLEGRINO trades those layers for constraints.

Tight memory. Single execution context. Direct buffer access. The constraints are the point.

This is not a bet on raw CPU power. It’s a bet that removing bloat gives you more than the hardware ever could. If the modern web had not committed the great sins of infinite memory sprawl and sluggish design, the machine would not have required a correction like PELLEGRINO to clear the glass.
